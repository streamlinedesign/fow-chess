# Changelog

All notable changes, newest first. `make release` turns Unreleased into a version.

## [Unreleased]

- War Mode has three clocks: 30 minutes plus 10 seconds a move (as before), 2 hours plus 30 seconds a move, and a day for every move. The home page and the desktop app pick one; the queue only seats players who picked the same clock, and all three count on the one War Mode rating. Players get 2 minutes to place their pieces, 5 in a two-hour game and a day in a daily one.
- Play a friend with a code can start War Mode: pick War Mode, create a code, and up to three friends join with it; bots take the seats nobody takes. Never ranked. Joining by code finds War Mode games too.
- Home page: the board always plays Solomon (or three of him in War Mode); the opponent picker is gone. Rex, Vercingetorix and Ramesses II have their own cards at the bottom, each opening a new game on the clock picked above. The v1.0 plans list is called Upcoming.
- Guests are told that wins unlock nothing until they log in: the bot cards and Solomon's line say "Log in, then beat it to unlock the set", and a guest's win on the home page board says so. Signed-in players still unlock galaxy by beating Solomon on the home page board, as before.
- Rex inutilis is a basic bot: he plays the classic pieces and beating him unlocks nothing. Holy, the set he wore, now unlocks on reaching a 2000 rating in either Eat the King or War Mode, and stays once reached (`rating:2000` in `unlocks`). Players who already beat Rex keep holy (migration 012).
- War Mode is back on the four-player cross (three rows each, the four middle squares give full vision); the extended board is a mod again. Ranked games, the home page board and the rules page all follow.
- The home page War Mode board works in turns: your move shows at once, the line under the board says whose move it is, and the bots show as thinking while they play. A player knocked out there keeps seeing the board live instead of jumping a few moves back. The move list keeps each player's moves in their own column when someone is out.
- War Mode takes turns again, Red, Blue, Green, Violet, in place of Sync Move: ranked games, the home page board against three bots, and the rules page. Sync Move stays a mod (Mods, Four players), and works with the extended board and Battleship there. Games already going keep the rules they started with.
- Signing out goes back to this browser's guest pieces and tiles (the defaults if none were picked) instead of keeping the account's, which made cyberpunk look like the guest default. Guest and account choices are stored apart; guest picks made before this are reset once.
- Fog is Dusk only: hidden squares stay under a dark veil tinted by their tile skin. Smoke and the fog setting are gone; the settings preview's Fog button still shows it.
- Four-player bots in the fog weigh their king's exposure less (0.5 pawns a way in, was 1.5): as strong, and bot-only games end far more often. The test of bots playing on after a player resigns no longer needs the game to end, only 400 moves, so it stops failing CI now and then. Bots in a four-player game stop moving once no person is left in it and nobody is watching, so a game of bots alone no longer runs on the server for good; anyone who opens it starts them again.
- Fog has two looks, picked in Settings: Smoke (the default), one soft cloud with grain over what you can't see, and Dusk, a dark veil that keeps each hidden square's colour and tile. Both take the colour of the tile skin underneath. Vision squares and the hill stay ringed above the fog. The fog style is kept with your preferences (`fog` in `/api/prefs`) and only shows on your screen.
- A crisper board: a double rule frame, square corners, slightly brighter squares, an outlined last move, darker move dots, corner wedges for captures and a small shadow under pieces.
- Settings has a live preview beside every setting (inside Pieces on narrower screens): a full board in both your sets on your tiles, with Swap sides and a Fog button to see your fog style.
- Tap an enemy piece you can see to see where it could move, in both games. The server works it out from the board as you see it (hidden squares count as empty, no castling or en passant), so it tells you nothing new. New `reach` field in the game state.
- The home page lists what's coming in "Upcoming", under About the game: rules may change, hand-drawn pieces and skins, apps for Windows, Linux, Mac, iOS and Android, a separate engine for self-hosting, more skins, tournaments, more mods, security and anti-cheat. The Opera Game replay is gone from the rules page, along with its data and test.
- War Mode's Play button is solid, like Eat the King's.
- War Mode rules: the Battleship setup card says you can tap or drag.
- Holy pieces: the halo and its rays fit inside the piece, so the queen's, king's and prince's halos are no longer cut off at the top. Cyberpunk king, queen and prince have no star.
- Four-player bots under fog: they guard their king against squares they can't see and take and hold their vision squares. With fog and vision squares, War Mode's bot won 30% of games against three that don't (25% is level).
- War Mode is played in the fog of war again, with centre vision: a piece on one of your two vision squares in the middle shows you the whole board. Ranked games and the home page board both.
- Four players: the extended board's neutral corners are plain squares, no stripes. Back row and Battleship setups take drags as well as clicks: drag from the tray onto a square, from square to square, or back onto the tray.
- The rules page has two sections: Eat the King rules (fog, eating the king, the king-in-danger warning, starting a match, clocks and rating, draws) and War Mode rules (the extended board, Battleship setup, Sync Move, jester pawns, last king standing, clock and rating). Back row, Battleship, Sniper Win and jester pawns as mods are explained where you pick them on the Mods page; the setup hints there now mention the Sniper Win.
- Home page: Eat the King and War Mode are marked as the official games, with an Official tag, an ink bar down the side and your rating in each. War Mode's button just says Play.
- Your ratings moved from the header into your menu, both of them: Eat the King and War Mode. `/api/me` returns `fourRating`.
- Settings has a cog icon; Mods has a dial.
- "Inspired by" no longer lists Hungry Hungry Hippos.
- Fixed the unlocks test, which still expected a new account to have no themes after Cyberpunk became everyone's for the beta (main was red).
- Bots: `bots.ThinkTime` caps the time a move takes (600 ms); 0 lifts it, so chess-bots can play a seed the same way every time.
- Stronger bots. One-against-one, the searching bots share a new search (iterative deepening, transposition table, captures played out) that looks only a few plies deep under fog, where a deeper look trusts a board missing every hidden piece. Solomon imagines where the pieces it can't see might be, many times over, and plays the move that does best on average. Bots use the king warning like a player does. In 400-game matches under Eat the King's rules each bot beat its old self: Vercingetorix by about 215 Elo, Ramesses II 110, Solomon 270; the ladder stays in order. Rex inutilis still plays at random. Four players: a new bot that plays Sync Move as it is (a piece is in danger on any square someone could move to this round, a piece that moves away dodges, two pieces landing together both go), with levels: Vercingetorix plays the old bot, Ramesses II and Solomon the new one, Solomon also lining up next round's captures. In War Mode Solomon wins 85% of games against three of the old bot (25% is level). `go test ./bots -run Strength` and `./four -run FourBotStrength` replay the matches.
- Cyberpunk king, queen and prince: the glitched star is now the galaxy's two sparkles in the same place and size, with a neon ghost beside them and a thin cut across, in place of the sliced, outlined star. Ancient pieces: the Greek key on each foot is centred on it (it sat to the left, most on the pawn and jester).
- Back to the default rules: Eat the King warns you when your king is attacked and has centre vision again, and `/rules` is one page. This undoes #45 (in 0.18.0, never released).

Everyone has them while the beta is open, guests included; after `BETA_ENDS` accounts made before then keep them. Drawn by `scripts/cyberpunk.py`.
